The CT Supralight centre field teaches the fuel valve and key interlock: the closed valve's handle covers the ignition key slot, and the key will not turn out of OFF until the valve is open; a refused turn names the fuel valve above the panel. Opened, the valve leaves the whole key switch free to turn. The Avionics Master is drawn larger than the other rockers, the ELT remote switch sits left of centre with its ON and ARM legends, the flap selector sits right of centre, and every centre-field control is at least a full touch target.
